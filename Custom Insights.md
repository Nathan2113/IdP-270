**Custom Reports**
Identity Protection > Overview > Reports
- can send these reports to third-party users
	- i.e. accounts with compromised passwords can be sent out to necessary users
- works really well with multi-domain tenants

### Custom Insights
Identity Protection > Inventory and Posture > Custom Insights

**Compromised Password Report**
Custom insight > gets used in custom report > send to users on a chosen cadence

![](assets/Pasted%20image%2020260903114342.png)

- will need other filters
	- exclude stale and inactive
	- can include other password risks (duplicated, no pass, etc)
